01 — Who I Am

About

I'm an Electrical & Automation Engineer based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. I work at Engineers & Engineers (Electricals) Pvt. Ltd., where I handle panel design, component sizing, and automation engineering.

My background spans relay logic circuits, VFD panel design, MCCB/contactor selection, and control transformer specification — with hands-on work on DOL starters, Star-Delta, and APFC panel protection schemes.

On the software side, I'm actively building PLC programming skills in Siemens TIA Portal (S7-1200), practicing with Factory I/O and PLCSIM Advanced. My long-term goal is industrial automation — PLC + SCADA, system integration, and eventually high-end sectors like pharma or oil & gas.

Self-driven. 10+ months of independent study before my first role. I don't wait for someone to teach me — I build the roadmap myself.
